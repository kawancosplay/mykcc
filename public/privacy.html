Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 15, 2026 • KawanCosplay Community Portal (MyKCC)

1. Introduction

KawanCosplay Group operates MyKCC (https://mykcc.vercel.app). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

2. Data We Collect

When you authenticate using Google Sign-In or fill in our registration form, we collect minimal data required to issue your digital member pass:

  • Google Sign-In Scopes: Full Name, Email Address, Avatar Profile Picture URL, and unique Google account UID.
  • Registration Data: Cosplay / Stage Name, Full Legal Name (confidential), City, Country, Primary Community Role, and optional social handles.
  • Gallery Uploads: Photos you choose to showcase in the community gallery.
Google API Services User Data Policy / Limited Use Disclosure:

MyKCC's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We do NOT sell, trade, or transfer your Google user data to data brokers or third parties.
  • We do NOT use Google user data for advertisements or marketing profiling.
  • We do NOT use Google user data to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

3. Data Storage & Security

Data is stored in Google Cloud / Firebase Firestore with AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 encryption in transit. Administrative data is guarded by role-based Firestore Security Rules.

Your Rights & How to Request Data Deletion:

You have the right to request deletion of your account and all associated personal records at any time. Simply send an email to cosplaysehat@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request" containing your registered email or 8-digit KCC ID. All data will be permanently wiped within 30 days.
